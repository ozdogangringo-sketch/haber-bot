Detailed justification for requested TikTok for Developers scopes for creators and editorial publishers.

Permission / Scope Requirement Level Technical Purpose Data & Privacy Policy
user.info.basic Required Verifies that the authenticated creator profile matches the authorized TikTok account and queries basic avatar/open_id metadata. No user personal data collected. Only the authenticated creator token is stored securely for scheduled publishing.

video.publish Required Directly posts the uploaded video bulletins to the public TikTok feed of the authorized creator with concise summaries and topical hashtags. Direct Post allows scheduled publishing synchronized with market opening/closing bells without manual mobile app delays.
